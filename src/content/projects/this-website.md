The site you're on right now.

I wanted a personal space on the internet that wasn't tied to any platform. Somewhere I owned. So I built one.

## What it is

A personal site with a blog, project log, and an about page. Static, fast, and minimal. No tracking, no ads, no algorithm deciding what gets seen.

## How it's built

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build) — a framework that's genuinely great for content-heavy sites. All posts are written in Markdown. The design is hand-rolled CSS with a retro terminal aesthetic, because I spend most of my time in terminals and I think they look great.

## What I learned

That I have strong opinions about typography. And that getting a blinking cursor to feel right takes longer than you'd expect.
