I have this habit of finding one song and playing it until it breaks.

Not literally — but until it stops feeling like a song and starts feeling like a memory. Like the music has worn a groove into something and I can't hear it anymore without that specific emotional residue attached.

It's kind of annoying. It means I go through phases where my playlist is basically one track on repeat, and then I feel vaguely sad when I've used it up.

## The songs that get used up

They're usually not the *best* songs I've ever heard. Sometimes they're almost embarrassingly simple. A chord progression I've heard a thousand times, a lyric that's almost a cliché. But there's something in the timing — the right song at the right moment — that fuses it to whatever was happening in your life.

That's the whole thing about music. It's a timestamp.

## What I'm listening to lately

I've been going deep on ambient stuff recently. Long tracks, no vocals, the kind of thing that sits in the background and slowly changes the texture of whatever you're doing. It's good for thinking. Good for not thinking.

There's something almost meditative about putting on a 40-minute drone piece and just letting it happen.
