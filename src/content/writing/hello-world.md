So I finally made a website.

I've been putting this off for a while. I kept thinking I needed a *reason* — like a job, or a brand, or some polished portfolio that would impress someone somewhere. But honestly, I just wanted a place that felt like mine.

Not a LinkedIn. Not an Instagram. Not a README.

Just somewhere I can write things down, share stuff I'm working on, talk about music or whatever's been on my mind. A small corner of the internet that's mine.

## What you'll find here

I don't want to put myself in a box. Some of this will be technical. Some of it won't. I might write about a project I built, or a song I can't stop listening to, or just something I've been thinking about.

No schedule. No niche. No content strategy.

Just things I find worth sharing.

## Why the terminal look?

Because I spend most of my day staring at one, and I think they're beautiful. There's something satisfying about monospace text and clean lines. It suits how my brain works.

Also, it's mine. And that's kind of the whole point.
